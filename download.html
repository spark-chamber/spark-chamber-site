Before you start

  • This is Early Access, so expect rough edges. More topics are on the way.
  • Updates keep your progress, and the app keeps automatic backups. To move your progress to another computer, use Settings → Export progress and Import progress.
  • Found a wrong answer or a bug? Use the Report a problem button in the app.
  • Hands-on builds use only battery or USB power, at 12 V or less. Read the safety notes before you build.

Release notes

0.5.2October 5, 2026
  • Theory shows each topic as a small drawing of its part or plot; its name appears when you hover over it or reach it with the keyboard.
  • Theory highlights your path's goal subjects and marks your next few topics.
  • The progress readout on Theory counts by subject: Core, Circuit analysis and Electronics.
  • Phones and narrow windows get the Theory map too, with pinch to zoom and tap to open.
  • The title screen greets you at every launch, with Continue first.
  • The calculator knows meters, coulombs and joules, and asks when a bare m could mean meters or milli.
  • In the final test, Formulas and the calculator open beside the problem, as in practice.
  • Esc closes the Esc menu reliably, wherever the keyboard focus is.
0.5.1October 4, 2026
  • Theory opens on your next topics at full size, with the whole core in view for a new student.
  • Press 0 for the whole map; press it again to return to your next topics.
  • A selected topic stays readable beside its card, at any window size.
  • Esc returns to Theory from a problem or Progress opened there.
  • Tab reaches the next topic on Theory, and Enter or Space opens it.
0.5.0October 4, 2026
  • A new look. Filament, in warm graphite and amber, is the default; Obsidian, Solder mask and Blueprint are in Settings, each in light and dark.
  • A title screen, then a choice of path: Electrical engineer or Maker. The other paths show the subject that opens them.
  • A clearer Theory map: the basics at the center, one area for each subject, and a selected topic shows everything it builds on.
  • Units can be typed the way you write them, such as 5 khz, 4.7 kohm or 12 volts, with unit buttons under the calculator.
  • Shift+Enter uses the calculator's result as your answer, and the calculator shows fractions and powers upright as you type them.
  • A missed problem comes back later with new values.
  • Estimates show the working: each number rounded to one figure and a power of ten.
  • Key terms appear in their subject's color, and conventional current is explained before it is used.
  • Report a problem: nothing leaves your device until you press Send on the form.
0.4.1October 3, 2026
  • Report a problem opens the report form in your browser, already filled in. The report is filed only when you press Send, and no account is needed.
0.4.0October 3, 2026
  • A design map: each location is a real circuit to design, build, and measure on the Bench, checked against its specification. Clearing one reveals the next.
  • The Workshop is a tree rooted at the Bench, with labs you run on the Bench step by step.
  • Terminology: the key terms on boards by subject, with the main ones on pages of their own.
  • A final test of three problems before a topic lights.
  • A better placement test, with an “I don't know” button, fairer scoring, and a retake from Theory or Settings.
  • Circuits on the Bench are kept with your progress, and you can save any circuit as a blueprint.
  • A browser version at app.sparkchamber.app, with Export and Import to move your progress between it and the app.
  • Faster Theory and Workshop maps.

Known limits: Workshop terms don't add tools to the Bench yet, and blueprints can't be placed into map locations yet. Full screen (F11) comes in the next version.
