  2. Open Spark Chamber from your apps.

Before you start

  • This is Early Access, so expect rough edges. More topics are on the way.
  • Updates keep your progress, and the app keeps automatic backups. To move your progress to another computer, use Settings → Export progress… and Import progress….
  • Found a wrong answer or a bug? Use the Report a problem button in the app.
  • Hands-on builds use only battery or USB power, at 12 V or less. Read the safety notes before you build.

Release notes

0.4.0October 3, 2026
  • A design map: each location is a real circuit to design, build, and measure on the Bench, checked against its specification. Clearing one reveals the next.
  • The Workshop is a tree rooted at the Bench, with labs you run on the Bench step by step.
  • Terminology: the key terms on boards by subject, each with its own page.
  • A final test of three problems before a topic lights.
  • A better placement test, with an "I don't know" button, fairer scoring, and a retake from Theory or Settings.
  • Circuits on the Bench are kept with your progress, and you can save any circuit as a blueprint.
  • A browser version at app.sparkchamber.app, with Export and Import to move your progress between it and the app.
  • Faster Theory and Workshop maps.

Known limits: Workshop terms don't add tools to the Bench yet, and blueprints can't be placed into map locations yet. Full screen (F11) comes in the next version.
