FEEDBACK

Report a problem or share an idea

Found a wrong answer, a confusing question, or a bug? Tell us here. You don't need an account.

What kind of feedback is it?

Say what you expected and what you saw. For an idea, say what it would help you learn or do.

Problem details from the app

Report a problem in the app fills these in by itself. They let us open the exact problem you saw.

Only if you'd like a reply. We use it for nothing else.

What happens next

  1. Your report is filed privately. Only the Spark Chamber team can read it.
  2. We check it against the app. With the problem details, we can reopen the exact problem you saw.
  3. We fix it in a release. If you left an email address, we'll tell you when the fix is out.

We store what you write and the problem details, but not your IP address. A Cloudflare check guards the form against spam. See the Privacy page.
