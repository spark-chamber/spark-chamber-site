Help test Spark Chamber 0.5

Spark Chamber is an electronics course for your computer: circuits, the physics behind them, a circuit simulator, and problems that ask why, not only what. This beta checks that it teaches well before a wider launch.

Dates

The beta runs from October 4, 2026 to [END]. The survey closes [END].

Start

  • Browser (any computer, including a Mac): app.sparkchamber.app. Your progress stays in that browser; Settings → Export progress saves a backup.
  • Linux: sudo snap install spark-chamber
  • Mac app: coming to the Mac App Store. Use the browser version for now.

What to try

About 2–3 hours over two weeks.

  1. Light the center ring. Open Theory (M) and work through the eight basics: charge and current, voltage and energy, resistance and Ohm's law, power and heat, sources, capacitors, inductors, and real components. Already know them? Take the placement and go further.
  2. Use the tools on something real, ideally something you're studying or building: the Simulator (C), the Calculator, and Terminology (G).
  3. Report what's wrong. Use Report a problem in the app for a wrong answer, a confusing problem or a broken screen, one report per problem.
  4. Take the survey around day 10. It's seven questions and takes about five minutes.

What's collected

The app collects nothing and sends nothing on its own. You send only what you choose: problem reports and survey answers, which go to a private repository. There's no account and no email address. See Privacy.
