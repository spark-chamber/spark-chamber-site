PRIVACY · LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 3, 2026

Your progress stays on your device

The Spark Chamber app collects nothing. It has no account, makes no network requests of its own, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, and any problem reports you save stay in the app's own storage on your device. The app never uploads them. What you can choose to share is described below.

  • In the Snap version, it's in ~/snap/spark-chamber/common/, a folder that every update shares, so updates can't erase it.
  • The app also keeps automatic backups of recent saves in the same folder.
  • In the browser version, it's in that browser's storage, on that device only. Another browser or device doesn't see it, and clearing the site's data deletes it. Safari deletes it after 7 days without a visit unless you install the app (Add to Dock or Add to Home Screen), so use Settings → Export progress to keep a copy.
  • Settings → Reset progress clears it. Uninstalling the Snap removes it too, though your system keeps a snapshot for 31 days unless you uninstall with snap remove --purge.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to share leaves your device, and the app itself never sends anything. The browser version loads only its own files from app.sparkchamber.app, and nothing from any other site. Export progress… (in Settings) writes your progress to a file you pick, and Copy progress backup copies it to your clipboard. Each goes only where you save or paste it.

Report a problem saves the report on your device and copies it to your clipboard. From version 0.4.1, it also opens the feedback form in your browser with the report filled in: your comment, the topic, problem, and seed, your answer, the expected answer, and the app version. The report travels in the form's web address, so this site's host, GitHub Pages, receives it when the page opens and may record it in its logs. The report is filed only when you press Send. If you close the tab instead, nothing is filed.

App stores

The store you install from (for example the Snap Store, run by Canonical) records installs and updates under its own privacy notice. Spark Chamber receives only the store's aggregate statistics, such as install counts by country. It never receives anything that identifies you.

This website

Apart from the spam check on the feedback page, this site has no cookies, analytics, or tracking. It and the browser version are hosted on GitHub Pages, and this site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web host, GitHub and Google see your IP address and browser details when you open a page. Their own policies apply: the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Feedback you send

When you send the feedback form, we store your message, the problem details, and your email address if you give one. They go to a private GitHub repository that only the Spark Chamber team can read. We don't store your IP address, and we use your email address only to reply to you. To have a report deleted, say so in a new report and include the email address you used.

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam, and a Cloudflare Worker passes your report to GitHub. Cloudflare processes these requests under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Children

Spark Chamber is meant for students, including minors. The app collects nothing, and the only data we keep is the feedback people choose to send. Younger students shouldn't include their email address.

Questions

Send them through the feedback form. The app's About page says the same as this page. If they ever differ, the app is right and this page will be corrected.
