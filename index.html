01 · HOW IT WORKS

Understand it, prove it, build it

Spark Chamber is for beginners, for students taking Circuits I or Electronics I, and for hobbyists who build things.

  1. Understand the ideaEach topic starts with the idea, why it happens, and a worked example.
  2. Practice until the topic lightsAnswer five problems in a row correctly, without hints, each harder than the last. Then pass a final test of three problems, with one try each.
  3. Build it and measure itWire the circuit on the Bench, predict a reading, and measure it with a multimeter or an oscilloscope.

No mascots, streak quests, or subscriptions. The challenge is the engineering itself.

03 · REAL WORK

Design to a spec, with real parts

Design problems give you a requirement and a range of standard part values. The app accepts any design that meets the requirement, not just one textbook answer. It runs some designs in a circuit simulator, so real effects such as op-amp bandwidth or an actual diode drop decide whether your design works.

  • Standard part values from the E12 and E24 series
  • Worst-case analysis with part tolerances
  • Datasheet limits: absolute maximum and recommended operating ratings
  • Designs checked in a circuit simulator
  • The Bench: wire a circuit, predict a reading, and measure it with a multimeter, an oscilloscope, a function generator, or a Bode plotter
  • Hidden faults to find with the meter, on circuits from the problems or your own
  • Circuits you can export to LTspice
  • A design map: each location is a real circuit to design, build, and measure against its specification, and clearing one reveals the next
  • Workshop labs that you run on the Bench, step by step
  • Saved circuits, and blueprints you can open again from your library
  • Build it for real: a breadboard layout and parts list for circuits powered by batteries or USB, at 12 V or less

Nothing explodes. If your design works only on paper, the feedback names the effect you missed.

04 · CORRECTNESS

Correct, or it doesn't ship

A wrong answer does more harm than a missing topic, so Spark Chamber checks every problem before you see it.

ComputedAnswers come from formulas. None are typed in by hand.
TestedEach problem type has a test against an answer worked out by hand.
Cross-checkedThe built-in simulator solves some circuit problems a second way, as an independent check.
ReviewedEach topic is reviewed against the sources it cites.

Found a mistake anyway? Use the Report a problem button under the problem. We read every report.
